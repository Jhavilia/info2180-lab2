Understand Gender Bias

October 6, 2026 by Kaddy Dean

Gender bias occurs when individuals are treated unfairly or held to different standards based on their gender. These biases are often rooted in deep-seated social stereotypes and cultural norms about how men and women are expected to act, work, or lead.

Addressing gender bias requires building awareness in our everyday environments, from schools to workplaces. By promoting equal opportunities and evaluating people on their actual abilities rather than stereotypes, we can create fairer communities for everyone.
